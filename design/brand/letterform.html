Runit or RunIt?

The mark puts a glowing lens where the I should be. Drawn as a tapering wedge it reads as a lowercase i with a big dot — so it spells Runit, while the brand rule says RunIt. Squaring the stem fixes the spelling. These are the live SVGs, beam animation and all.

The two candidates

as drawn Runit

Wide at the baseline, tapering to a point. That silhouette is a light fixture on a stand, and the ball above it is unmistakably a tittle — which is exactly why it reads lowercase.

chosen RunIt

A uniform bar from cap height to baseline, square at both ends, lens recentred over it. A capital I wearing a dot no capital should have — and the light is the reason it has one.

What sits behind this

The wordmark survives icon sizes — and the repo believed it wouldn’t.

The current icon was chosen partly because a wordmark supposedly couldn’t work small. That confused points with pixels: a home screen renders 180 px, where the wordmark is crisp.

commit 8f97859 — "It also survives being 40px on a home screen, which a wordmark would not." home screen 60 pt → 180 px on 3× spotlight 40 pt → 120 px settings 29 pt → 87 px notification 20 pt → 60 px
Cropping to the spotlight alone doesn’t work.

The obvious idea is an icon that is just the lamp. But the lamp is tall and narrow, so no square crop holds it without clipping it or dragging in its neighbours.

lamp bounding box 97 × 392 units square crop ≥ 392 → imports the n and the t result → reads "nIt" A lamp-only icon must be NEW artwork, not a crop.
The static renders have to wait for the beam to settle.

The PNGs the app ships are stills of this animation. Capture too early and you get a mid-swing beam and a dim lens — a wrong icon that looks perfectly plausible.

SMIL: dur=6.4s repeatCount=1 fill="freeze" capture at t=7.5s verified, not assumed: t=0 render 621,790 bytes settled 628,666 bytes identical: false
Only the drawing changes. No theme colour moves.

The amber lives in the brand artwork alone. It never enters the app’s token table, so no screen changes colour.

design/theme.css unchanged src/theme/tokens.ts unchanged tokens.test.ts unchanged squared variant geometry: stem x 754–820, y 367 (cap height) → 650 (baseline) lens cx 824 → 787, recentred over the stem clears the t, whose crossbar starts at x 833.6

Decided

The squared variant. It spells the name the brand rule asks for, and the trick survives — a capital I has no business wearing a tittle, so the glowing one reads as deliberate rather than as punctuation. Cleaner silhouette at 60 px too, where the wedge starts merging into the n.
