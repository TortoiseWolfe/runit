RunIt wireframes

Each journey is drawn twice: what a person gets today, and what it should be. Grey boxes only. This is about layout and flow, not colour or brand. The changes are proposals for you to accept or reject before anything is built.

The rules behind every proposed screen

  • One screen, one job. If a screen needs a scroll to finish its job, its job is too big.
  • The person's own thing is always visible. Their photo, their request, their nickname. Never “trust us, it went somewhere”.
  • Empty space must be earned. Content sits under the thumb, and leftover height goes to the content, not to margins.
  • A disabled-looking control is a bug. A live primary button looks live.

Open questions for you

  • Photos: is a guest's own pending photo visible only to them (proposed), or should hosts be able to release it from the album view too?
  • Landing tab: after joining, a guest lands on Chat, which is read-only. Should a first-time guest land on Photos, the one tab where they can act?
  • Scope: I drew Join, Photos, Create and the Host console. Music and the sign-in screen are not covered yet.
  • Fidelity to the design canvas: these differ from the original design. Should the original design be updated first, or should these replace it?
