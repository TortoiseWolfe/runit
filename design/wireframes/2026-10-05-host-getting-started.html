  • The two-doors join screen (#98) is the bigger fix; this is the half that ships now.

What ticks each item

  • Invite your guests: invitedCount > 0 or guestCount > 0. Sharing from the share sheet leaves no record, so a guest arriving is what proves it worked.
  • Post a first announcement: the event has at least one broadcast.
  • Add the plan for the night: the run of show has at least one row.

Not in this

  • An intro carousel. The trouble testers had was before the app (Apple's email, TestFlight), and the help page at /help/ covers that.
  • A guest checklist. A guest's only hard step is the join, which happens before the app.
  • Ticking "showed the QR": opening a sheet is not an outcome, and nothing records it.
