How RunIt works

One screen for a party: the host's announcements, a shared photo album and song requests. Guests need no account and no phone number.

Open RunIt in this browser

Join a party

Nothing to install. It works in your phone's browser.
  1. Open the link the host sent you, or scan the QR code they're holding up.
  2. Type your name and tap Join. If the code isn't filled in for you, the host has it: six letters and numbers.
  3. You're in. Chat has the host's announcements and the plan for the night, Photos is the shared album, and Music is where you request songs and vote for other people's.

Get the app

Optional. RunIt is in beta, so it isn't in the App Store yet.

The browser does everything above. Pick your phone:

iPhonethrough Apple's TestFlight
  1. Send the host the email address on your Apple ID. They add you by hand.
  2. Apple emails you an invitation from App Store Connect. It doesn't mention parties, and it often lands in Junk or Spam. Search your email for App Store Connect, not TestFlight.
  3. Open it on your iPhone and tap Accept. The invitation runs out after about three days. If it has, ask the host to send another.
  4. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It's Apple's free app for testing.
  5. Open TestFlight, signed in with the same Apple ID. RunIt is in the list. Tap Install.
Androida direct download
  1. Open the host's invitation link on your phone and tap Android · install now.
  2. Allow the install when Android asks. It's about 130 MB, so use wi-fi.

Host a party

A few minutes, from the app or this website. No account needed.
  1. Open RunIt and tap Running an event? Make one.
  2. Fill in your name, the party's name and the date. A time, a place and a doors line are optional. Tap Create it.
  3. Save your recovery key. It's shown once, and it's the only way back into your party from another phone. Copy it into a note or text it to yourself, then tap I have saved it.
  4. Invite people. Share invite sends the link and the code from your phone. Under Event, Who is invited adds people one at a time, from your contacts, or from a saved list.
  5. At the party, tap Show QR and hold your phone up. Guests scan it to join.
  6. Then Broadcast sends announcements, DJ queue runs the song requests, and Photos is where you approve pictures, if you've turned approval on.

A new party holds 10 guests. During the beta you can raise that for free: Event, then Change plan.
